## Validation limits

Automated checks cover geometry, collisions, the requested relative elevation order, destination safety, ascending/dipping roads, connecting paths, walls, bamboo, covered walks, steps and open-ground tree exclusion. The browser available for development lacks WebGL: the 2D fallback can be checked, but the final 3D appearance and first-person motion must still be checked on a WebGL-capable device.
